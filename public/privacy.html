Privacy

Starlight Fishing Club · last updated October 20, 2026

The short version

You can play without an account. Your club is saved in your browser on your device. If you choose to sign in, we keep a copy of your save so you can play on another device. We do not sell anything about you, show ads, or track you around the web.

Without signing in

Your progress is saved only in your browser (local storage) on your device. Nothing about your game is sent to us.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. You can use Google or a sign-in link sent to your email. We then store:

  • your email address and an account id, so you can sign in again;
  • your game save and a small summary of it (which water your club is at, how many fish you have caught, your coins), so your club follows you to other devices.

If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address and basic profile (name and picture). We only use the email address to sign you in.

Who stores it

Accounts and cloud saves are stored by Supabase, our database provider. The game itself is served by Cloudflare. Each player's save can only be read by that player: the database checks who is signed in before sharing any save.

Your choices

  • Delete your cloud save or delete your account at any time in the game: Settings, then Account. Deleting your account removes your email and your cloud save. Your save on your own device stays unless you also reset it there.
  • Export your save any time in Settings to keep your own copy.
  • Sign out whenever you like. The game keeps working without an account.

Children

The game is fine for all ages. If you are under 13, please play without signing in, or ask a parent first.
