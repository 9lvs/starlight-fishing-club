- Notable: tests run in a single thread because worker start-up is very slow in the sandbox.

Bought Cork Grip in the browser and saw the bite window change.

## Phase 4: Waters, map, time of day, weather
- Built: map feature listing every water from `waters.json` with unlocked / locked ("Needs River Charts", derived from data) / coming soon states, "Sail here", and an unlock toast when `boatRange` crosses a water's `rangeRequired`; per-water scene presets (rolling hills, pines, cliffs; reeds, rocks, lilies) and per-phase palette tints from data; 10-minute day cycle (day, dusk, night, dawn) driven by the wall clock with an 8 s Bayer-dither palette dissolve; deterministic weather per 3-minute period (clear, gentle rain, fog) changing the fish pool and visuals (rain drops and splashes, drifting dithered fog, dimming, cloud cover); stars that twinkle, a moving sun and moon with water reflection, fireflies at dusk and night, occasional shooting stars.
- Verified: a water and a fish added only as JSON (`tests/extensibility.test.ts`) appear, unlock with boat range, emit `waterUnlocked`, and become the fishing conditions. Also demonstrated in the browser with a temporary `src/content/zz_demo/` pack: "Lantern Lake" loaded with its own pine skyline and fish and could be sailed to, then the pack was removed. 50 tests, typecheck, and build pass on the committed tree.

## Phase 5: Crew, auto fishing, offline progress
- Built: crew feature (crew slots from the Hire Help upgrade, three data-driven helper types with hire cost, speed, value, and luck; assign each helper to any unlocked water; live progress bars; live rolls every `crewIntervalMs x intervalMult` that go to the journal and sell automatically at `crewSellMult` = 50%; crew never use pity, so hand fishing keeps better odds); offline catch-up in the core (`TickDriver` detects any gap over 5 s: closed tab, background tab, sleep, or debug fast-forward, and caps it by `offlineCapHours`, 8 h base, up to 24 h with Cozy Hammock); `systems/offlineSim.ts` computes rolls per time-of-day/weather segment with closed-form counting, exact per-fish chances, coins as the exact expected value, and fish counts by largest remainder; "While you were away" summary with the capped-time note; pending toast/panel queue so requests made before the HUD exists are not lost.
- Verified: in the browser, a hired Deckhand plus a 10 minute clock skip gave 49 rolls plus one carried to the next tick (50 = 600 s / 12 s), and a 2 hour skip gave exactly 600 catches and +4,439 coins (7.4 per roll, matching the expected value at 50% crew value); the summary screen showed it. Tests prove: 10 min = 50 rolls with no leftover, progress carries in and out, coins equal the segment-by-segment expected value over 3 h, roll counts match a brute-force 1 ms loop, 24 h with three crew is deterministic and fast, and the cap is applied. 59 tests, typecheck, and build pass on the committed tree.

## Phase 6: Save system and settings
- Built: settings feature (sound on/off, volume steps, reduced motion with System/On/Off, export save, import save, reset save with a tap-again confirmation, credits); export/import through a small palette-styled HTML dialog with copy-to-clipboard (canvas text cannot be selected); `M` toggles sound. The save system (built in Phase 1) has a root version with root migrations, per-slice versions and migrations, defaults merged into older data, untouched storage of slices from removed features, backup of unreadable saves, autosave every 15 s plus on tab hide and page close, and a lock after reset/import so a pending autosave cannot undo them.
- Verified in the browser: changed coins, journal, settings, and creel, reloaded, and everything matched; a garbage code and a code with a damaged checksum were both rejected with friendly messages while the stored save stayed byte-for-byte identical and saving kept working. Tests cover round-trips, default filling, the v0 flat save to v1 slices, fishing slice v1 to v2, newer-version refusal, checksum failure, malformed payloads, reset backups, and the post-reset write lock. 71 tests, typecheck, and build pass on the committed tree.

## Phase 7: Audio, mobile polish, performance, accessibility
- Built: Web Audio manager (no Phaser) that never plays before the first tap or key press, suspends while the tab is hidden, caps master volume for calm, loads real files from `public/audio/` when listed in `src/config/audio.ts`, and otherwise plays soft pentatonic synth placeholders (cast, bite, reel, catch, coin, purchase, unlock, UI click, five rarity stings that grow with tier) over a quiet filtered-noise water bed; bus-to-sound wiring in `core/audioWiring.ts`; settings drive mute and volume. Mobile: fills the phone at integer scale, safe-area insets from `env(safe-area-inset-*)` move the HUD and menu, no pinch or double-tap zoom, no overscroll, no long-press callout, all buttons have hit areas of at least 24 game pixels. Accessibility: reduced motion (System/On/Off) stops parallax drift, bobbing, shooting stars, sparkles, palette dissolves, and panel slides; rarity is shown by shape and pip count as well as color; affordable upgrade nodes get a coin mark, locked nodes a lock.
- Verified: frame cost in the browser (WebGL) about 0.07 ms per update+render step with rain or fog, a dither dissolve step about 0.9 ms (every 500 ms), and a full layer repaint about 10 ms once per time-of-day change. Audio tests confirm it is silent and safe before a gesture and that every rarity sting in content has a sound. 74 tests, typecheck, and build pass on the committed tree.
- Only you can verify: how the sounds feel, and real phone behavior (notch, home bar, touch feel).
